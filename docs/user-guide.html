GOG User Guide

Global Opportunity Gateway turns real challenges in Kigali into teams, and teams into working projects.

The journey: Challenge, Application, Selection, Team, Private Project Workspace.

Getting started

  1. Open https://gog.gania.tech and choose Create a free account.
  2. Enter your name, email and a password, then choose participant or challenge owner.
  3. Open the confirmation link sent to your email, then log in.
  4. Complete your profile: bio, skills, education, location, interests and global experiences.

For your safety, you are logged out after 30 minutes without activity, and an account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed log in attempts.

Guide for participants

Find and apply to a challenge

  1. Open Challenges. Search by keyword, or filter by sector, skill, deadline, status or verified organisations.
  2. Open a challenge and read the problem, skills needed, expected outcome, deadline and team size.
  3. Apply with a short motivation. You can apply once to each challenge, and not to your own.

Follow your application

StatusMeaning
SubmittedThe owner has not opened it yet.
Under reviewThe owner is looking at it.
AcceptedThe owner chose you. An invitation may follow.
RejectedThe owner chose other applicants.
WithdrawnYou took the application back before a decision.

Join a team

  1. Open Invitations, then confirm or decline.
  2. When the owner forms the team, your private workspace appears in Projects.
  3. Use the tabs for tasks, research notes, files (up to 10 MB), discussions and milestones.

Guide for challenge owners

Post a challenge

  1. Confirm your email and complete your profile.
  2. If you represent an organisation, register it and upload supporting documents. Once verified, your challenges show the Verified badge.
  3. Open Post a challenge, add the details, then save a draft or publish.

Choose your team

  1. Open My challenges and review applications. You see an applicant's full profile after they apply.
  2. Accept or reject each applicant, then send invitations to accepted people.
  3. When at least one person confirms, form the team. A private workspace is created.

Run the project

  1. Assign team roles, then add tasks and milestones.
  2. Follow progress, which is calculated from completed tasks and milestones.
  3. Mark the project complete when the work is finished.

Your account and privacy

  • Other users see only your name and skills. Challenge owners see your full profile after you apply, and teammates can see it too.
  • Use Forgot password on the log in page if you cannot sign in.
  • You can delete your account from My profile. This cannot be undone. If you own an active project, complete it first.
  • Use Report on a challenge or account that breaks the rules, for example by asking participants to pay.
